A FEW THINGS YOU MAY WONDER

Let’s clear things up.

Can you help with a small balcony or terrace?

Yes. Balcony gardens, terrace gardens, vertical gardens and kitchen gardens are part of GIC’s listed services. Share your space and requirements to discuss a suitable approach.

Do you offer maintenance after installation?

GIC lists annual maintenance contracts, mowing, fertilizing, pruning, lawn maintenance and irrigation system maintenance. The team can discuss the right scope for your garden.

How much will my landscape cost?

Pricing depends on area, design, plants, materials, access and ongoing care. Share your project details to request a tailored quotation. Construction follows approval of the detailed quotation.

What kinds of projects do you work on?

The existing GIC portfolio includes residential, commercial, hospitality, international, public and campus categories.
